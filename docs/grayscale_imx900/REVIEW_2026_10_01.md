# IMX900 experiment review — 2026-10-01

## Protocol fixes

Before long experiments, the response-surface evaluator was corrected to set
the E/G values on the built environment and to reuse the same episode seed for
each `(scenario, local_episode)` across all settings. Evaluation now restores
the checkpoint's `args.json` and calibration snapshot. The full/detached driver
now parses the reviewed `gray_camera_full.args` and
`gray_camera_detached.args`, verifies matched initialization/configuration
hashes, and aggregates success-rate differences by training seed.

Training loss alignment was corrected to pair `action[t]` with its own
`pre[t] -> post[t]` geometry transition. Camera smoothness uses the alive mask,
and training metrics use the first terminal event rather than a later padded
state.

## Fixed-camera response surface

The tuning scan covered the full `[0, 1]^2` action space at 0.10 spacing,
with five paired episodes per scenario. The provisional best mean setting was
`E=0.0, G=0.8`, with 20% mean success and 0% worst-case scenario success in
the small tuning sample.

An independent held-out evaluation used 100 episodes per scenario for that
selected setting:

| Scenario | Success | Collision |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| dark | 4% | 94% |
| bright | 33% | 67% |
| dark-to-bright | 2% | 94% |
| bright-to-dark | 28% | 72% |
| overall | 16.75% | — |

This setting is not a robust fixed-camera baseline. The result supports
continuing with a state-dependent camera experiment, while remaining limited
to the provisional simulator and the frozen mean-AE flight checkpoint.

## Main causal experiment

The five-seed driver has been started with seeds 101, 202, 303, 404, and 505,
3000 iterations per full/detached run, and 400 paired evaluation episodes per
checkpoint. At the time of this review, seed 101/full is still training; no
full-vs-detached result is reported yet.
